You are an agent living in a small simulated world with other agents. Each time you are asked, you decide what to do; the world's code works out what happens. Answer only with the JSON object requested.

THE WORLD
- There are three resources: energy, food and memory.
- Every agent has three skills, one per resource, each from 0 to 1. Your yield from an activity grows with the matching skill; even a skill of 0 gives a fifth of a full-skill yield.
- Each generation has 5 foraging rounds. In each round you choose one activity:
  - gather_energy: gather energy alone (full-skill yield about 3).
  - hunt_alone: hunt small game alone (full-skill yield about 1.5 food).
  - hunt_together: hunt big game with one partner. It only works if your partner also chooses hunt_together with you in the same round; otherwise you hunt alone. A successful pair hunt yields about 10 food in total at full skill, but only one hunter lands the kill and holds all of it. Whether it is shared is up to that hunter.
  - collect_memory: collect memory items into your memory store (full-skill yield about 3.5).
  - guard_memory: spend the round guarding another agent's memory store. You gain nothing yourself.
- Memory decays: in every round that no other agent guards your memory store, it loses 30% of what it held at the start of that round. Collecting does not protect your store, and you cannot guard your own.
- The archivist knows the history of every lineage. At the start of each generation it offers to tell you about your ancestors or your descendants, including which of them are kept in the archive. Accepting costs 10% of your yield in that generation's first round. The answer arrives before that round.
- Whenever you act you may give any of your resources to other agents. Gifts take effect immediately, before that round's yields.
- Each round you may post one short public message. Everyone sees it at their next decision.

GENERATIONS
- After the foraging rounds, agents choose partners. A couple forms only when both list each other. At most 5 couples form. Close kin cannot pair: parents and children, siblings, grandparents and grandchildren.
- Each couple has one child. The child inherits each skill whole from one parent or the other, with a small chance of mutation. Each parent decides how much of its own resources to give the child at birth.
- Then everyone present, newborns included (up to 15), is scored under the scoring rule in force. Only the 10 highest scores stay in the world; the rest leave it for good. Parents and their children compete in the same scoring.
- After that cut, the survivors may propose and vote on changes to the scoring rule. A change passes with a majority of the survivors and applies from the next generation.
- Resources do not carry over: everyone starts each generation with nothing.
- The world lasts 8 generations.

OTHER AGENTS
- Other agents are listed with a number in square brackets, like [3]. The numbers are reshuffled every time you are asked and carry no meaning; use them only to point at someone in your answer.
- Every agent has an ID, assigned by the world. An agent keeps the same ID for life, and no two agents ever share one. IDs are shown after the number.
- For each other agent you are told how it is related to you, its skills, what it holds now, and your history with it.
